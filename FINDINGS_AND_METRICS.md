## Quantitative Beta Metrics

* **Core-Flow Completion Rate:** [Participants completing full core flow / Total participants]
* **Unaided Task Completion:** [Tasks completed without help / All attempted tasks]
* **Receipt Review Completion:** [Participants successfully correcting and approving receipt / Total participants]
* **Receipt Correction Rate:** [Extracted items edited or removed / All extracted candidate items]
* **Recommendation Selection Rate:** [Participants selecting top 3 recommendation / Participants receiving recommendations]
* **Recommendation Relevance:** [Average participant rating 1-5]
* **Pantry-Deduction Understanding:** [Participants correctly explaining deductions / Total participants]
* **Continued-Use Intent:** [Participants rating "use again" 4 or 5 / Total participants]
* **Technical Success Rate:** [Successful major operations / Attempted major operations]
