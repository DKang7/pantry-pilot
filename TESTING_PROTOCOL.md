# User Testing Protocol

## Moderator Instructions
* Tell the user: "We are testing PantryPilot, not you. There are no wrong actions."[cite: 3]
* Do not immediately rescue a confused participant; ask neutral questions like "What are you looking for?"[cite: 3]

## Core Tasks
1. **Understand the Product:** Look at the landing page and explain what you think this product does[cite: 3].
2. **Access Account:** Sign in and reach the pantry dashboard[cite: 3].
3. **Import Receipt:** Add the groceries from this receipt to your pantry[cite: 3].
4. **Review Extraction:** Review the extracted information, correct one item, remove a non-food item, and add a missed item[cite: 3].
5. **Approve:** Finish adding the correct items to your pantry[cite: 3].
6. **Manage Pantry:** You used two eggs this morning. Update your pantry[cite: 3].
7. **Request Recipe:** Find a dinner recipe that takes no more than 30 minutes and requires no more than two missing ingredients[cite: 3].
8. **Review Recommendation:** Open the recipe that looks most useful and explain why you selected it[cite: 3].
9. **Simulate Cooking:** Imagine that you cooked this recipe. Update PantryPilot accordingly[cite: 3].
10. **Privacy:** Find out how you would remove the uploaded receipt image[cite: 3].

## Task-Completion Scale
* **Completed unaided:** Participant completed the task without help[cite: 3].
* **Completed with minor prompt:** Participant needed one neutral reminder[cite: 3].
* **Completed with major help:** Participant needed direct instruction[cite: 3].
* **Failed:** Participant could not complete the task[cite: 3].
* **Technical failure:** Product failure prevented completion[cite: 3].
