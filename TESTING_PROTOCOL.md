# User Testing Protocol

## Moderator Instructions
* Tell the user: "We are testing PantryPilot, not you. There are no wrong actions."
* Do not immediately rescue a confused participant; ask neutral questions like "What are you looking for?"

## Core Tasks
1. **Understand the Product:** Look at the landing page and explain what you think this product does.
2. **Access Account:** Sign in and reach the pantry dashboard.
3. **Import Receipt:** Add the groceries from this receipt to your pantry.
4. **Review Extraction:** Review the extracted information, correct one item, remove a non-food item, and add a missed item.
5. **Approve:** Finish adding the correct items to your pantry.
6. **Manage Pantry:** You used two eggs this morning. Update your pantry.
7. **Request Recipe:** Find a dinner recipe that takes no more than 30 minutes and requires no more than two missing ingredients.
8. **Review Recommendation:** Open the recipe that looks most useful and explain why you selected it.
9. **Simulate Cooking:** Imagine that you cooked this recipe. Update PantryPilot accordingly.
10. **Privacy:** Find out how you would remove the uploaded receipt image.

## Task-Completion Scale
* **Completed unaided:** Participant completed the task without help.
* **Completed with minor prompt:** Participant needed one neutral reminder.
* **Completed with major help:** Participant needed direct instruction.
* **Failed:** Participant could not complete the task.
* **Technical failure:** Product failure prevented completion.
